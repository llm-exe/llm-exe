## Summary

- Fixed #772: the Gemini `effort` transform gated `thinkingConfig.thinkingBudget` behind a literal allowlist of three `gemini-2.5-*` model ids, so `effort` was silently dropped on every current shorthand (`google.gemini-3.1-flash-lite`, `3.5-flash`, `3.5-flash-lite`, `3.6-flash`, `3.7-flash`).
